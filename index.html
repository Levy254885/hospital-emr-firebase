Company Overview

Design thinking, careful materials, professional delivery.

Executive Interior Design (EID) is a Kenya-based interior design brand dedicated to transforming residential, commercial and hospitality environments into beautiful, practical and memorable spaces.

We combine creative design thinking, careful material selection and professional project coordination — so every space reflects the people who use it and delivers lasting value.
