Our Story

Interiors that reflect the people who use them.

Executive Interior Design (EID) is a Kenya-based interior design brand dedicated to transforming residential, commercial and hospitality environments into beautiful, practical and memorable spaces.

Our approach combines creative design thinking, careful material selection and professional project coordination — because we believe an exceptional interior should do more than look good. It should reflect the personality of its users, support the way people live and work, and deliver lasting value.

From concept development to finishing details — flooring, tiling, blinds, wall finishes, kitchens, roofing and swimming pool solutions — we bring style, functionality and attention to detail into every project under the promise captured in our tagline: Beauty in Style.
